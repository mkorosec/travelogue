# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Casual travelers — people who dream about and plan trips during downtime.
They open the app on their phone to mark where they've been, browse the map,
check stats, and get inspired for the next destination. Usage is spontaneous,
lightweight, and aspirational.

## Product Purpose

Travelogue is a digital scratch map. It replaces the paper poster on the wall
with an interactive, always-with-you world map. Mark countries as visited or
want-to-go, watch your progress grow, and share your travel footprint.

## Brand Personality

Wanderlust, refined, warm. Three words: **aspirational**, **elegant**, **tactile**.

The app should feel like picking up a beautifully printed atlas — substantial,
detailed, something you want to spend time with. Dark mode by default (like
browsing maps by lamplight), but warm rather than cold.

## Anti-references

- Not a sterile corporate dashboard with tables and chart widgets
- Not a flashy gamified app with coins, XP bars, achievement popups, or
  notification spam
- Not a generic SaaS app with blue gradients and hero metrics
- Not Google Analytics for travel

## Design Principles

1. **Map-first** — the map is the hero; everything else is secondary support.
   The stats sidebar should feel like notes in the margin of an atlas.
2. **Zero friction** — no onboarding, no accounts, no setup. Open and interact
   immediately. First tap on a country should work.
3. **Tactile feedback** — every interaction should feel physical: smooth
   color transitions, satisfying stat counters, responsive hover states.
4. **Mobile-native thinking** — the phone is the primary device. Design for
   thumb reach, small screens, and portrait orientation first. Desktop is a
   bonus expansion.
5. **Quiet confidence** — the design doesn't need to shout. Let the map data
   do the talking. Restrained color palette with purposeful accents.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum for contrast ratios
- All interactions work with keyboard and touch
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for animations
- Color is never the sole indicator of state (use icons, labels too)
